Our Story

About Utsav

Est. 2009

Built on a Foundation of
Trust & Elegance

Utsav was founded in 2009 with a single vision — to provide the families of Baramati with a venue worthy of their most cherished moments. Positioned at the iconic Satav Chowk, we have since grown into the most trusted celebration venue in Pune district.

Our name, Utsav, meaning festival and celebration in Sanskrit, is a promise we renew with every event we host. From intimate engagement ceremonies to large wedding receptions hosting over a thousand guests, our dedicated team ensures flawless execution every single time.

Over 15 years, we have hosted more than 500 events — each one unique, each one unforgettable. We believe that behind every great celebration is a great venue, and we are proud to be that venue.
