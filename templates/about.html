Built for
Memorable Gatherings

Located at {{ site.address }}, Utsav Banquet Hall is a fully air-conditioned venue crafted for intimate weddings, receptions, birthdays, family functions, and corporate events.

Our venue accommodates a maximum of 200 guests, with a garden lawn for up to 75 guests and an AC dining area for 75 guests per sitting (larger gatherings dine in batches).

Included in the rent: chairs, garden lawn, full air-conditioning (hall & dining), generator back-up (diesel charges extra if used), kitchen for your caterer (pure vegetarian food only), sound system with speakers, separate washrooms for ladies and gents, and a common changing room. Food, beverages, and decoration are arranged by the customer.
