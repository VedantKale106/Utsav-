1. Venue, Capacity & Facilities

  1. Maximum 200 guests. No guest above 200 will be allowed entry. This limit is for safety and will not be relaxed.
  2. Garden lawn: maximum 75 guests.
  3. Dining: maximum 75 guests at one time. Larger gatherings must dine in batches.
  4. The venue is for intimate weddings, receptions, birthdays, family functions and corporate events only.

Included in the rent

  • Chairs
  • Garden lawn (up to 75 guests)
  • AC dining area (75 guests per sitting)
  • Full air-conditioning (hall and dining)
  • Generator back-up (diesel charges extra if used)
  • Kitchen (for the customer's caterer)
  • Sound system with speakers
  • Separate washrooms for ladies and gents
  • Common changing room

Not included (customer must arrange)

  • Food / catering
  • Beverages
  • Decoration

  2. Extra hours are given only if the next slot is free, and never beyond 10:00 PM.
  3. Decoration, set-up and clearing must be done within the booked slot. Time used before or after the slot is charged as extra hours.

3. Booking & Payment

  1. 100% of the rent must be paid at the time of booking. No date is blocked on part payment, token amount or verbal promise.
  2. The booking is confirmed only when (a) full rent and security deposit are paid, and (b) the terms form is signed.
  3. The booking must be in the name of the person hosting the event, with a copy of photo ID (Aadhaar / PAN / driving licence) and address.
  4. The purpose of the event must be written on the booking form. The venue cannot be used for any other purpose.
  5. The booking cannot be transferred or sub-let to any other person.
  6. Payment by cash (within legal limits), UPI, cheque or bank transfer. A cheque booking is confirmed only after clearance; a bounced cheque cancels the booking.

4. Security Deposit

  1. Refundable security deposit: Rs. 10,000 (Rupees Ten Thousand only), payable with the rent.
  2. Refunded within 7 working days after the event, only to the person who booked, by bank transfer.
  3. Deductions for damage, extra hours, extra cleaning or any breach of these rules will be made from the deposit. If the deposit is not enough, the customer must pay the balance before leaving.

  1. Cancellation is valid only if given in writing (letter, email or WhatsApp to the booking number). The date of receipt decides the refund.
  2. One date change is allowed free if informed at least 15 days in advance, subject to availability. A second change is treated as a cancellation.
  3. The security deposit is refunded in full on cancellation.
  4. If the venue must cancel due to reasons beyond its control (natural calamity, government order, etc.), the full amount paid will be refunded or the event shifted to another date.

6. Food, Catering & Kitchen

  1. Utsav Banquet does not provide food or beverages. The customer must bring their own caterer.
  2. Only pure vegetarian food is allowed. Non-veg food, eggs included, must not be brought, cooked or served anywhere on the premises.
  3. The caterer's name and phone number must be given at least 3 days before the event.
  4. Gas cylinders, stoves and cooking only in the kitchen, never in the hall, dining area or garden.
  5. Utensils must not be washed in the hall, dining area or garden.
  6. The caterer must hand back the kitchen clean and remove all food, waste and material before the slot ends.
  7. The management is not responsible for the quality, quantity or hygiene of food served by the customer's caterer.

7. Alcohol, Smoking & Prohibited Items

  1. Alcohol is strictly prohibited anywhere on the premises (hall, dining area, garden, kitchen, washrooms, parking).
  2. Smoking, gutkha, tobacco and any intoxicating substance are strictly prohibited.
  3. Weapons of any kind are not allowed.
  4. Firecrackers, sky lanterns, open flames, confetti guns and inflammable items are not allowed without written permission.
  5. Single-use plastic and thermocol (plates, cups, cutlery, carry bags) are not allowed, as per the Maharashtra plastic ban.
  6. Gambling, political gatherings and any unlawful activity are not allowed.
  7. Pets are not allowed.

8. Music & Sound

  1. The venue's sound system may be used. The customer may also bring a DJ.
  2. All music, DJ and loudspeakers must stop at 10:00 PM sharp, as per the Noise Pollution (Regulation and Control) Rules, 2000.
  3. Volume must stay within permitted limits. Staff will lower or switch off music if neighbours complain or police direct.
  4. Any music licence (PPL / IPRS) or police permission is the customer's responsibility.

9. Decoration & 10. Safety

  1. Decoration is not included. The customer may bring their own decorator.
  2. No nails, screws, tape, glue, pins or paint on walls, ceilings, doors, pillars or furniture.
  3. Decorators must clear all material before the slot ends.
  • Fire exits, extinguishers and passages must never be blocked.
  • Customers/vendors must not touch electrical panels, AC controls, generator or wiring.
  • Children must be supervised by parents at all times, especially in the garden.
  • Management may refuse entry or stop any unsafe or unlawful activity.

11. Parking & 12. Conduct & Responsibility

Parking: Parking for 10 four-wheelers and 50 two-wheelers only, first come, first served. Extra vehicles must park outside without blocking the road or neighbours' gates. Parking is at the owner's risk.

  1. The customer is personally responsible for the behaviour of all guests, caterers, decorators, DJs and other vendors.
  2. The customer must ensure no quarrel, breach of peace or unlawful act takes place in or around the venue.
  3. Police/other permissions for baraat, procession, band, road use or drone shooting must be taken by the customer.
  4. The customer must pay the full cost of any damage to the building, furniture, AC, lights, sound system, washrooms or garden.
  5. Management is not responsible for loss or theft of jewellery, cash, gifts, vehicles or personal belongings. Articles left behind are kept for 7 days only.
  6. Management may photograph the decorated venue (without guests) for promotion, unless the customer objects in writing.
